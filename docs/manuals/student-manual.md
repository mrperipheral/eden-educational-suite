# Student Guide

A simple guide to using your student portal.

---

## Login

1. Open the school's system web address.
2. Click **Sign in**.
3. Type the **email address** and **password** your school gave you.
4. Click **Sign in**.
5. You'll land on your **Dashboard**.

**Trouble logging in?** Use **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page. Still
stuck? Ask your school office.

## Dashboard

Your dashboard shows your name, admission number, current class, session
and term, plus quick links into **Results**, **Attendance**,
**Assignments**, and **Timetable**. Across the top of every portal page
you'll also see tabs for every section you have access to (only sections
your school has switched on will appear).

## Timetable

Click the **Timetable** tab to see your weekly class schedule (only shown
if your school uses this feature).

## Assignments

Click the **Assignments** tab to see homework set for your class — subject,
due date, and whether your teacher has marked it submitted, late, or
exempt.

## Assignment submission

There is no file-upload button in this version — your teacher marks your
submission status themselves once you've handed in your work the way your
school normally collects it (in person, etc.). The **Assignments** tab is
where you check that status, not where you submit files.

## Learning materials

Click the **Learning Materials** tab to see files (PDF, images, audio)
your teachers have shared with your class. Click one to view or download
it.

## Attendance

Click the **Attendance** tab to see your attendance record — how many
days you were present, absent, or late this term.

## Results

Click the **Results** tab to see your published results by term —
subject scores, grade, and (if your school uses it) your class position.
**You will only ever see a term's results after your school has
published them** — if a term doesn't appear yet, it's simply not ready.

## Report cards

Click the **Report cards** tab to view your full, formatted report card
for a published term — the same one your school prints. Use your
browser's print/save option to keep a copy.

## CBT examinations

**Purpose:** Sit an online exam your teacher has scheduled for your class.

**Steps:**
1. Click the **CBT** tab. You'll see every exam for your class, marked
   **Open** (available now) or **Completed** (you've already taken it).
2. Click an open exam → you'll see its subject, number of questions,
   duration, and the time window it's available in → click to begin.
3. Once you start, a countdown timer begins at the top of the screen —
   **watch it**; the exam auto-submits itself the moment time runs out,
   even if you're not finished.
4. Numbered circles let you jump straight to any question — a circle
   turns green once you've answered it, so you can see at a glance what's
   left.
5. Pick your answer for each question — **your answer is saved
   automatically the moment you select it**, you don't need to click a
   separate save button per question.
6. Use **Previous**/**Next** to move between questions, or click a number
   to jump directly.
7. When ready, click **Submit examination**. If anything is unanswered,
   you'll be warned and asked to confirm before it submits.

**Important:**
- You can only attempt each exam **once**.
- You cannot go back and open the exam again once you've submitted (or
  once the timer runs out).
- The system — never your browser — decides your score. You cannot see
  the correct answers to any question during or after the exam.

## Released CBT results

Your score appears on the **CBT** tab / the exam's own result page —
**but only once your school releases it**. Some exams release results the
moment you submit; others release on a scheduled date your teacher sets.
If your result isn't showing yet, it means it hasn't been released yet —
check back later, or ask your teacher when it will be.

## Fees

Click the **Fees** tab to see what's been charged to you, what's been
paid, and what's still outstanding. If your school accepts online payment
and you owe a balance, you'll see a **Pay online** button — click it,
enter an amount, and complete payment on the secure payment page that
opens. You'll be brought back automatically once it's confirmed.

## Profile / security

1. Click your name (top-right) → **My Profile** / **Account settings** to
   see your details.
2. To change your password, use the password section there.
3. Click your name → **Sign out** when you're done, especially on a shared
   computer.

**A note on privacy:** you only ever see your own information — never
another student's results, attendance, or fees.
