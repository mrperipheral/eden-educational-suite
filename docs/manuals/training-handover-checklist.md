# Training Handover Checklist

For the salesperson/trainer to work through with a newly onboarded school.
Tick each item only once you've actually watched it happen, not just
explained it. Cross-reference the relevant manual for detail on any item.

**School name:** _______________________  **Date:** _______________________
**Trainer:** _______________________  **School contact:** _______________________

## Role training completed

- [ ] **School Admin trained** — walked through
      [School Administrator Manual](school-admin-manual.md) sections 1–14
      at minimum (login, dashboard, settings, academic setup, module
      activation).
- [ ] **Teacher trained** — walked through the
      [Teacher Manual](teacher-manual.md), with at least one teacher
      taking attendance and entering a real score themselves.
- [ ] **Accountant trained** — walked through the
      [Bursar Manual](bursar-manual.md), with the bursar recording a real
      payment themselves.
- [ ] **Parent onboarding explained** — School Admin knows how to link a
      guardian to a student and assign the Parent role; a parent's login
      shown working.
- [ ] **Student onboarding explained** — School Admin knows how to link a
      student's account and assign the Student role; a student's login
      shown working.

## Configuration completed

- [ ] **School settings configured** — profile, branding (logo), regional
      preferences saved.
- [ ] **Academic setup completed** — at least one session (marked
      current), at least one term, levels, arms (if used), and subjects
      created; subjects offered at the right levels.
- [ ] **Modules activated** — reviewed the full module list together;
      confirmed which are on/off matches what the school actually wants
      to use.
- [ ] **Users created** — School Admin knows how to add a member and
      assign a role from **Members** → **Add member**.
- [ ] **Students imported/created** — at least a representative sample of
      real students added and enrolled in their classes.
- [ ] **Teachers created** — real teachers added, with their teaching
      assignments (class + subject) set.
- [ ] **Fees configured** — at least one fee category and fee structure
      created.

## Workflows tested end-to-end

- [ ] **Timetable configured** (if the school uses it) — at least one
      class's weekly schedule built and published.
- [ ] **Attendance tested** — a real register created, marked, and
      submitted by a Teacher account (not just School Admin).
- [ ] **Results workflow tested** — an assessment created, scored,
      published, locked; a result run compiled through to Publish/Lock;
      a report card opened and confirmed it looks right.
- [ ] **Parent Portal tested** — logged in as (or watched) a real parent
      account; confirmed they see their own child(ren) and nothing else.
- [ ] **Student Portal tested** — logged in as (or watched) a real student
      account; confirmed they see their own data and nothing else.
- [ ] **Payment workflow tested** — a manual payment recorded by the
      bursar; if Paystack is configured, one online payment tested
      end-to-end.
- [ ] **CBT tested** (if the school uses it) — one exam created,
      scheduled, and sat by a test student account; result release
      behaviour (immediate or scheduled) confirmed to work as expected.
- [ ] **Reports tested** — the school has opened the Reports area and
      confirmed real numbers match what they entered.

## Handover confirmed

- [ ] **School understands support process** — they know who to contact
      and how, if something goes wrong or they have a question.
- [ ] **School received user manuals** — links or copies of the
      [School Administrator](school-admin-manual.md),
      [Teacher](teacher-manual.md), [Bursar](bursar-manual.md),
      [Parent](parent-manual.md), and [Student](student-manual.md) manuals
      handed over.
- [ ] **School completed acceptance checklist** — the school's own
      representative has signed off that the system does what was
      demonstrated and agreed.
