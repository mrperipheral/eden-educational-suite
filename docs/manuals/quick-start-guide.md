# School Quick-Start Guide — First Week With the System

*"What should we do first?"* This sequence follows the platform's actual
dependencies — each day's work builds on what you set up before it. It
assumes your School Admin account is already created and you can log in.
For full detail on any step, see the
[School Administrator Manual](school-admin-manual.md).

Skipping ahead is possible but not recommended — e.g. you cannot enroll a
student before a level exists, and you cannot take attendance before
students are enrolled.

---

## Day 1 — Login & foundation

**Goal: your school "exists" in the system with a working academic
structure.**

1. **Log in** with the account/credentials given to you.
2. **School configuration** — go to **School settings**:
   - **Profile** tab: fill in contact email/phone, address.
   - **Branding** tab: upload your school logo (used on printed report
     cards later).
   - **Regional** tab: set your timezone, currency, and date format.
   - **Modules** tab: review what's on by default and switch on anything
     you know you'll need this week (e.g. **Timetable** or **CBT** if
     you plan to use them — they're off by default).
3. **Academic setup**:
   - **Academic** → **Sessions**: create your current academic
     session/year, mark it **current**.
   - Click **Terms** on that session → add your term(s).
   - **Academic** → **Levels**: create every class/year group your school
     has.
   - For each level: open **Arms & subjects** → add arms/streams if you
     use them, and tick which subjects each level offers (create subjects
     first under **Academic** → **Subjects** if you haven't).

**By end of Day 1:** your school has a name, a logo, a current session and
term, and a full list of classes and subjects.

---

## Day 2 — People

**Goal: your staff and students exist in the system, and are placed in
classes.**

1. **Teachers** — go to **Teachers** → **Add teacher** for each staff
   member. Add their **teaching assignments** (which class + subject each
   one teaches) from their profile — this is what scopes a Teacher-role
   user to their own classes later.
2. **Students** — go to **Students** → **Add student** for each student
   (or batch through them one at a time — there is no bulk import in this
   version). For each one, open their profile → **Add enrollment** to
   place them in a class.
3. **Guardians** — go to **Guardians** → **Add guardian**, then link each
   one to their child(ren) from the student's profile.

**By end of Day 2:** every teacher, student, and guardian exists, students
are placed in classes, and teachers know which classes they teach.

---

## Day 3 — Classroom operations

**Goal: prove the daily-use features actually work.**

1. **Timetable** (if you enabled it Day 1) — build at least one class's
   weekly schedule and **publish** it.
2. **Attendance** — as a Teacher (or School Admin), create a register for
   a real class and date, mark it, and **Save & submit** — confirm it
   locks correctly.
3. **Assessments** — under **Assessments** → **Categories**, set up your
   categories (e.g. Classwork, Test, Exam) if you haven't already. Create
   one real assessment, enter scores, **Publish**, and **Lock** it.

**By end of Day 3:** you've proven a Teacher can take attendance and enter
scores for a real class.

---

## Day 4 — Money & communication

**Goal: fees are configured and staff can log parent communication.**

1. **Fees** — go to **Fees** → **Categories**, add your fee types.
   **Fees** → **Fee structures** → **Create** to set the amount(s) for
   this term/level. Raise a real charge for at least one student and
   record a test payment to confirm the workflow end-to-end.
2. **Communication** — confirm the **Notifications** module is on. Log a
   test communication thread, and publish a test **Announcement** to
   confirm staff/parents will see it.
3. **Learning materials** (if you enabled it) — have a teacher upload one
   real file for their class.

**By end of Day 4:** money and communication are flowing, not just
configured.

---

## Day 5 — Results, assessments to grade, and reporting

**Goal: close the loop — prove a result can be produced and reported on.**

1. **Results** — under **Results** → **Grading schemes** and
   **Weighting schemes**, set these up if you haven't. Create a result
   run for a class with locked, published assessments, **Compile** it,
   walk it through **Mark reviewed → Approve → Publish → Lock**, and open
   a report card to confirm it renders correctly with your logo.
2. **CBT** (if you enabled it) — create one real examination, add
   questions (or pull from the Question Bank), **Schedule** it, and have
   a test student account sit it to confirm the whole flow.
3. **Reports** — visit **Reports** and open a few areas (Academic,
   Attendance, Fees) to confirm real numbers show up, matching what you
   entered this week.

**By end of Day 5:** your school has a complete, real, end-to-end proof
that every layer works — structure, people, daily operations, money, and
results/reporting.

---

## After week one

- Set up **Member accounts** and roles (§12 of the School Admin Manual)
  for every staff member who needs to sign in themselves.
- Link guardian and student accounts to their own logins so the **Parent
  Portal** and **Student Portal** are actually usable, not just
  configured.
- Review the [Training Handover Checklist](training-handover-checklist.md)
  with your trainer/salesperson to confirm nothing was missed.
