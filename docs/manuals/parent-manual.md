# Parent Guide

A simple guide to using the parent portal — no technical knowledge needed.

---

## How to log in

1. Open the school's system web address in your phone or computer's web
   browser.
2. Click **Sign in**.
3. Type the **email address** and **password** your school gave you.
4. Click the blue **Sign in** button.
5. You will be taken straight to **My Children**.

**Trouble logging in?** Click **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page and
follow the instructions sent to your email. If that doesn't work, contact
your school office — they may need to check your account.

## Dashboard (My Children)

This is the first page you see. It shows a card for each of your children
who is linked to your account, with their name, admission number, current
class, and a **View profile** button. If you have more than one child at
the school, they'll all show here.

**Don't see your child?** Contact your school office — they need to link
your child to your account before you can see anything.

## Choosing a child

If you have more than one child, open any child's page and use the
**Switch child** button (top-right of that page) to jump between them —
you never need to go back to the dashboard each time.

## Results

1. From **My Children**, click **View profile** on your child, or open
   their page and click the **Results** tab.
2. You'll see a list of terms with published results.
3. Click into a term to see subject-by-subject scores, grade, and (if your
   school uses it) class position.

**Note:** You will only ever see results your school has **published** —
if a term's results aren't ready yet, that term simply won't appear here.
This is normal, not an error.

## Report cards

1. Open your child's page → **Report cards** tab.
2. Click a term to view the full, formatted report card — the same one
   your school prints, including your child's photo of grades, comments,
   and (if enabled) signatures.
3. To keep or print a copy, use your browser's own print/save option.

## Attendance

Open your child's page → **Attendance** tab. You'll see a summary of days
present, absent, and late for the current term. Only **finalized**
(submitted) attendance registers are counted.

## Assignments

Open your child's page → **Assignments** tab. You'll see homework set for
your child's class, with the due date and whether it's been marked
submitted, late, or exempt by the teacher. You cannot submit work here
yourself — this is a status view only.

## Timetable

Open your child's page → **Timetable** tab to see their weekly class
schedule (only shown if your school has switched this feature on).

## Fees

1. Open your child's page → **Fees** tab.
2. You'll see everything charged to your child, anything already paid,
   and what's still **Outstanding**.
3. If your school accepts online payment and there's a balance owing,
   you'll see a **Pay online** button.

### Paying online

1. Click **Pay online**.
2. Enter the amount you want to pay (it can be less than the full balance
   — partial payments are accepted).
3. Click through to the secure payment page (Paystack) and complete your
   card/bank payment there.
4. You'll be brought back automatically once payment is confirmed, and it
   will appear on your child's fee page right away.

**Trouble paying?** If the payment page doesn't load or your payment
doesn't show up after a successful charge, contact your school office with
your payment reference — do not pay twice.

## Payment history

The same **Fees** tab lists every payment ever made for your child,
including the date, amount, and method — scroll down below the balance
summary.

## Communication

You will see school-wide **Announcements** addressed to parents (in the
navigation menu) and your own **Notifications** (a bell/inbox icon-style
link) for things directly relevant to you. You cannot start a new
conversation with the school from here — for that, contact the school
office directly (phone, email, or in person) as you normally would.

## Profile / security

1. Click your name (top-right corner) → **Account settings**, or
   **Profile** in the menu, to check the contact details the school has
   on file for you.
2. To change your password, go to your account settings and use the
   password section.
3. To leave, click your name → **Sign out**.

**A note on privacy:** you will only ever see your own linked children —
never another family's information, under any circumstance.
